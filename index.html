Before the drop

Five things, none of them long. Do them before 9/19/2026 so you're not sorting them out while everyone else is moving.

  1. Roll a neutral character.Neutral-only season, one character per challenger, no alts. Don't use the Jobe Backyard entry-hall glitch — it can flip your side and it breaks two of the org challenges.
  2. Read #challenges-rules, then the primer.#challenges-rules is the standing ruleset. The primer is the Season 12 layer on top. Nothing is hidden inside the roleplay posts — those are story and status readouts, not rules.
  3. Install the plugins.Get Code's AOSharp all-in-one from Season 12 #information. Unzip it, run aosharp.exe, load AO and your character, then tick ironman plugin and ironquestie and inject.
  4. Set your Discord nickname to your character name.Org chat gets loud and leadership asks for this so they can tell who's speaking.
  5. Turn on notifications for #announcements.Raids, cap raises and votes get called there, and the population is global — a cap-raise raid can land at any hour for you.

The rules

Full text is in #challenges-rules. Organised here by what you're most likely to trip over. The default position: assume everything is locked until you know otherwise.

The four that catch people.

1. Screenshot rare loot in the corpse or chest, before you loot it, and post it in #screenshots. Unverifiable items can be taken off you. Org-bot rolls are the exception.

2. Cancel buffs you didn't earn. Friendly outsiders will buff you; it's on you to drop anything your character can't cast and the org hasn't unlocked.

3. Nothing comes in from outside. No alts, no mains, no outside friends. You also can't loot a mob or mission no challenger pulled.

4. Don't out-level the cap. Going over earns a penalty.

Playing your character
  • Play your own character yourself, on a standard client. Nobody else plays it, you don't play theirs, and no using another challenger as a pocket.
  • No automation tools. Mission rollers are the exception.
  • No wall hacks, teleport hacks or anything that fundamentally changes the game.
  • Powerleveling only if you're a full cap below the current cap minimum, and only with explicit leadership approval.
  • No political, racist, homophobic or otherwise bigoted character names.
Gear and credits
  • Everything must be farmed, earned or given to you by another challenger.
  • You may give and trade gear with other challengers freely.
  • No bugs, exploits or third-party utilities to acquire or duplicate anything.
  • Promo items, Funcom Item Shop items and Daily Reward items are locked unless the season rules unlock them.
  • When a privilege is unlocked, that item may come from anywhere including an alt. Leadership often keeps a supply.
  • Unlocking an item in the Iron Shop lets you buy it there. It does not unlock the privilege for it.
Buffs
  • You may only use buffs the challenge has unlocked, or buffs your own character can cast.
  • A buff counts as unlocked when a challenger casts it without a wrangle or umbral wrangler and posts the execution text in #unlocked-buffs.
  • Agents may not unlock other professions' buffs.
  • Only the Borealis buff bots follow challenge restrictions. The OA bots don't — don't use them.
Raids
  • All raids are open to all challengers unless the format is team-limited (12man, ToTW instance).
  • Loot rolls randomly through the org bot, only for people present, and only if they were there more than half the raid.
  • Roll only on what you'll use. No reserved items for anyone or any profession.
  • Announce raids in org chat. Don't run one quietly.
Season 12 specifics
  • One character, no alts. You can switch and carry earned gear and credits across, but the old character leaves the org. Iron Questie progress survives.
  • Tradeskills: outside tradeskillers are limited to the unlocked skill level. Challenger tradeskillers are not.
  • Org votes run inside Iron Questie and you must be logged in to vote. The rules post says 24 hours, the primer says about 10 — watch #announcements.
  • Opting out of Questie is allowed, but there's no alternative route to Iron Coins, Chips or shop items.
  • OFAB and Serpentine are not restricted this season. If you played a previous season, this is the change most likely to catch you out.
Violations and penalties

Don't accuse anyone publicly or privately. Open a ticket on Discord with as much detail as you can. Penalties range from an uncomfortable conversation up through cap reductions, forced rerolls, item removal, losing buff and warp privileges, or removal from the season and the Discord.

Reference

Special equipment slots

You may equip items from the special list up to the number of unlocked slots. One item is one slot — two Dogs of War is two slots, twinking included. Two slots unlock per cap; a Cap 6 mission removes the limit. Adventurers, Doctors, Enforcers, Fixers, Nano-Technicians and Soldiers get a permanent +1. Cap 1's two come from Sephirah: Kingdom I (kill Abmouth in 15 seconds) and Sephirah: Foundation II (kill Aztur in 15 seconds).

OFAB and Serpentine are not on the restricted list this season, unlike previous ones.

Iron Coin Shop

Coins buy items, chips buy credits. About 2,500 coins come out of normal missions across the whole season, so budget. Items still have to be unlocked by an org mission before you can buy them.
